New system update

  1. Overpaying lowers the next installment

    • Anything paid past what an installment owes comes off the next ones, in order
    • The payment is still recorded as received
      • For example, 12,000 paid on a 10,000 installment: it shows -2,000 owed, and the next owes 8,000
    • Editing or deleting the payment updates the next installments by itself

  2. Client Thai ID

    • Entered on the client form, checked for 13 digits and a valid number
    • An ID already used by another client cannot be saved
    • Shown on the client page

  3. Change one installment

    • Click an installment in the schedule to change its due date and amount
    • The other installments stay as they are
    • Late fees count from the new due date

System addition

  1. More on the calendar

    • Adds down payment parts, insurance fees, police fines and car insurance expiry
    • Each day counts every kind on its own
    • The day, week, month or year on screen is shown beside the Calendar title
    • The list below is split by kind; click a heading to hide or show it

  2. Edit a client or car in a pop-up

    • No page change, the same as adding one

  3. Error codes

    • Every error message ends with a code, such as (E1003); give it when asking for help
    • A screen that breaks shows the error and a Try again button instead of freezing

    Log

    • The program keeps a log of what went wrong, on this computer only
    • Settings > Data > Open log folder, to send the file to whoever looks after the program

Visual adjusting

  1. The down payment's late fee sits under the installment late fees

    • Shows 0.00 when there is none

  2. A new contract opens the full down payment page straight away

    • No short form to fill in first

  3. Updates page

    • Settings > About > What's new in each version
    • The first time a new version opens, it shows what changed, once

Other

  • General and Minor bug fix

Caution

  1. Past overpayments now lower the next installment

    • If more than an installment owed was ever recorded, such as 12,000 on 10,000 to cover a late fee too, the extra 2,000 now comes off the next installment
    • Balances on existing contracts will change after the update

  2. Down payment late fees on older contracts

    • An older contract with no down payment on file gets one, due that day, the first time its down payment page opens
    • The late fee counts from that day; if the real due date was different, change it under Due date

  3. Back up before updating

    • Settings > Data > Backup now
    • The program backs up by itself each time it opens, but a backup of your own just before updating is safest
